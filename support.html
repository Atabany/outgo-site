Reminders

  • Reminders are local notifications. If none arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Outgo in iOS Settings, and that Focus modes aren't silencing them.
  • They fire at the time set in Outgo's Settings, the chosen number of days before each renewal, trial end or installment.
  • Outgo cannot cancel a subscription or a trial for you — cancel it with the provider, then mark it cancelled in Outgo.

Purchases

Outgo Pro is sold through the App Store as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Restore it on another device from Settings → Restore purchases. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
